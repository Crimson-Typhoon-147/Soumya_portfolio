01 — Physical entropy → secure messaging

EntropyLane

A security research project exploring whether physical traffic-motion dynamics can become a measurable source of cryptographic entropy. EntropyLane evaluates Shannon and Min-Entropy, conditions the signal, and derives message-specific AES-256-GCM keys.
